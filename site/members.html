Why we built Habing Lahi

Stark Industries uses technology to keep culture alive and to carry it further.

A festival lasts a weekend. A weaving pattern lives in one person’s memory. When the information sits only in a town, it reaches only that town. We put it online, in plain words, with the place and the people named, so a student in Manila or a family abroad can find it, and the town gets visitors who already know what they are looking at.

Our logo is a banig mat folded into an eight-ray sun. The site is built the same way: the islands as one woven map, the traditions as threads across the year, and the five of us as five threads in the same cloth.
