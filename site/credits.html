The recordings come from Freesound. We trimmed, looped, mixed to mono and levelled each one for the Overview; where we found no openly licensed recording of a festival, a parade in Calapan or Baco stands in. The swoosh is a sound made in code, from the earshot sound bank (MIT). The kulintang by Lake Lanao is made in code too: eight gongs, an agung, a babendir and a dbakan, built from their partials.
