# **<u>RVA FINAL REQUIREMENT: Digital Interface Design Project</u>** 

**Output:** 

1. Link to the web interface (functioning or prototype). 

2. PDF file containing: 

   - Short write-up (1–2 pages). 

   - Photo documentation (screenshots of the interface with captions). 

# **Instructions for Students** 

**1. Form Groups** 

   - 7 Groups with 5 members 

   - Create a Logo with Tagline ( combination of students name in a group) 

   - Assign roles: designer, researcher, writer/presenter, etc; 

**2. Choose Theme** 

# **Default Theme** 

Personal Portfolio Website 

- Showcase artworks, projects, or creative identity. 

- Sections: Homepage, Gallery/Works, About Me, Contact. 

- Narrates _personal identity_ and growth as an artist/student. 

# **Alternative Themes (Examples)** 

**_We will discuss these themes in our next meeting, but you may start researching the following examples in advance._** 

# **Advocacy Website** 

- Focus on a social issue, such as climate change, gender equality, or cultural heritage. 

- Narrates collective identity and promotes awareness and activism. 

# **Cultural Heritage Website** 

- Highlights Filipino art, traditions, cultural practices, or festivals. 

- Narrates community identity and celebrates shared heritage. 

# **Social Media / NFT Showcase** 

- Presents a mock-up of a platform featuring memes, NFTs, or digital art. 

- Explores contemporary identity in digital spaces. 

# **Public Narratives Website** 

- Documents murals, monuments, or art installations within the community. 

- Narrates shared stories and experiences expressed through public spaces. 

# **Political and Social Art Archive** 

- Curates artworks that respond to political or social issues. 

- Narrates collective struggles and voices through artistic expression. 

**3. Design the Interface** 

   - Create a functioning prototype (if skilled) OR a non-functioning mock-up (Figma, Canva, PowerPoint, etc.). 

   - Apply elements of art (line, color, texture, perspective, form/shape, space). 

   - Apply principles of design (balance, rhythm, emphasis, harmony, proportion). 

**4. Photo Documentation** 

   - Take screenshots of the interface (homepage, gallery, about page, etc.). 

   - Insert them into the PDF with short captions explaining design choices. 

**5. Short Write-Up (1–2 pages)** 

   - **Introduction:** chosen theme and purpose of the interface. 

   - **Analysis:** how elements and principles of art were applied. 

   - **Reflection:** how the interface narrates identity or collective stories. 

# **6. Compile into PDF** 

- Combine the write-up and photo documentation into one file. 

- Title the file clearly (e.g., _GroupName_InterfaceProject.pdf_ ). 

# **7. Submit Requirements** 

- Upload the link to the web interface. 

- Submit the PDF file with write-up + photo documentation. 

- Prepare a 10–15 minute group presentation on **_November 23, 2026._**
